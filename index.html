How to convert text to handwriting

  1. Paste your text into the box. Use # Title for a heading, **word** for a different pen colour and ==word== for a highlighter.
  2. Choose your language and handwriting. 35+ languages and 57 handwriting fonts, from neat student print to joined cursive, Urdu Nastaliq, Hindi, Bangla, Chinese and more. You can also upload your own handwriting font.
  3. Choose the paper — classic notebook, Pakistani register with Date / Page No., exam answer sheet, practical file, A4, graph, maths copy, 4-line English copy, Cornell notes and more.
  4. Add your header (name, roll number, class, subject, teacher, date) on the first page, on every page, or as a separate cover page.
  5. Download PDF, images or print. Every page is previewed before you download.

Why the pages look real

Each letter is written with small natural changes in size, angle, position and ink pressure, so the same letter never looks exactly the same twice. Lines drift slightly like real handwriting, ink soaks into the paper, and the optional scan and phone-photo looks add paper grain, lighting and shadow. Press Re-write to get a fresh variation of the same text.

FAQ

Is the text to handwriting converter free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is generated inside your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

How do I print the assignment?

Click Print, choose A4 paper and set margins to “None”. For best quality pick HD under “Scan / photo look” first.

Can I make more than one page?

Yes. Long text flows onto new pages automatically, or type --- on its own line to start a new page. Even 100+ page research papers work: import a .txt or Word .docx file, and download all pages or just a range.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the converter works on phones, tablets and computers.
